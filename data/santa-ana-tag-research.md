## Key Insights

- **Diaspora market**: ~2.4M Salvadorans in US (LA, DC/MD/VA, NYC). High-value audience.
- **Hispanic Heritage Month** (Sept 15 - Oct 15): seasonal spike opportunity
- **Title-tag relationship**: 2026 Etsy SEO says tags should expand coverage, not duplicate title words
- **"Salvadoran" vs "Salvadorian"**: "Salvadoran" is the correct demonym
- **Avoided terms**: "scale model" (too generic), "travel gift" (too broad), "latin america gift" (same problem as central america)
